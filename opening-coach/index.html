How to read chess moves

3...Bc5 means: on move 3, Black moves a Bishop to the square c5.

The number is the move number. Each move has a White turn and a Black turn.
3. (one dot) is White's move. 3... (three dots) is Black's move.

The letter is the piece: K king, Q queen, R rook, B bishop, N knight. No letter means a pawn: e4 is a pawn going to e4.

The square: letter a–h is the column, number 1–8 is the row. They're printed along the edge of the board. Row 1 is White's side.

x = takes. Bxc6: the bishop takes on c6. exd5: the e-pawn takes on d5.
+ = check. # = checkmate.
O-O = castle on the king's side. O-O-O = castle on the queen's side.

Each opening has several lines, one for each way the other player can answer. "Two Knights: When Black plays 3...Nf6" means this line starts when Black moves a knight to f6 on move 3.
